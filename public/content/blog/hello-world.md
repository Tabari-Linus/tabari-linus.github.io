This is the first post on the new site. I'm rebuilding my portfolio in the open, and this post is a small marker of that beginning.

## What lives here

Notes on backend engineering — mostly Spring Boot, distributed systems, and the small architectural decisions that separate systems that stay standing from systems that don't. Some notes on teaching too, because that's how I spend a good part of my time.

Nothing here will be long unless it needs to be. Nothing here will be short if it deserves depth. The signal I'm optimizing for is: *did I learn something specific, and can I say it clearly?*

## Why this exists

Two reasons. One, writing is how I test whether I actually understand something. If I can't explain why a decision was the right one, I probably haven't earned the decision yet. Two, I've been on the other side of enough hiring processes to know that a portfolio without written thought reads as a résumé with links. The writing is the difference.

More soon.
